Asked every week

Straight answers about mess, timing, and money.

Will there be mess?

Yes. Doorways are sealed with zipped dust screens when we work indoors, floor protection goes on every route through the house, a dumpster sits on the driveway, not the lawn, and there is a sweep-down at the end of every working day.

Will it run over?

Most jobs finish on the written schedule. Weather, hidden damage, and made-to-order delays are the honest exceptions. You hear from your site lead within 24 hours with the revised date in writing.

How do payments work?

Staged in the contract: 10% to book, 30% on start, 30% at mid-point, 25% at finish work, and the final 5% only after the punch list is cleared at sign-off.

Do we have to move out?

Usually not. Kitchens and bathrooms stay livable with planning. We will tell you plainly at the site visit if your job is the exception.
