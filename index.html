What we will not do

  • Open a wall before the scope is agreed.
  • Quote a remodel or addition from photos alone.
  • Hide material allowances inside a vague line item.
  • Start work that needs a permit without pulling one.
  • Cover up a problem found behind a wall without telling you.

The paperwork

One page, line by line, before any work starts. If something is not on the list, it is named in the exclusions on the same page.

  2. 02Cabinets, tile, and fixtures named, or an allowance shown
  3. 03Demolition and haul-away spelled out
  4. 04Framing, drywall, and finish work listed room by room
  5. 05Permit, plan, and inspection costs when the job needs them
  6. 06Which trades are involved and who coordinates them
  7. 07Anything that could change the price, flagged up front
  8. 08What is not included, in writing
